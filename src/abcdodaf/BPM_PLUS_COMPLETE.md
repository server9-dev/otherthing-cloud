## 🎯 **WHAT IS BPM+ TRIPLE THREAT?**

The **BPM+ Triple Threat** refers to the three core Object Management Group (OMG) standards for complete business process management:

1. **BPMN 2.0** (Business Process Model and Notation)
   - Structured process flows
   - Workflow orchestration
   - ISO/IEC 19510 standard

2. **DMN 1.3** (Decision Model and Notation)
   - Business decision logic
   - Decision tables with hit policies
   - FEEL expression engine

3. **CMMN 1.1** (Case Management Model and Notation)
   - Adaptive case management
   - Knowledge-intensive processes
   - Dynamic workflow support

Together, these three standards provide **complete coverage** for:
- Structured processes (BPMN)
- Business decisions (DMN)
- Adaptive cases (CMMN)
